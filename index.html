Is Octobo free?

Yes. You can download Octobo for free and study with two starter packs, flashcards, smart reviews, streaks and quizzes with friends. Octobo Plus unlocks every word pack.

How does the free trial work?

Eligible new subscribers can try Octobo Plus free for 7 days. The trial becomes a paid monthly or yearly subscription unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

Which languages does Octobo support?

The app is available in English, Spanish, Turkish, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil) and Japanese. Menus and word meanings use your language; English words and examples stay in English.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

No. Sign in with Apple or Google and your learning progress syncs securely to your account, so you can pick up where you left off.

How do I cancel Octobo Plus?

Open your Apple account settings and go to Subscriptions. Deleting your Octobo account does not cancel a subscription.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your synced learning data and cannot be undone.

Are the SAT and IELTS packs official?

No. They are unofficial study resources and are not affiliated with or endorsed by their respective trademark owners.
